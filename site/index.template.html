Why Brewer

Everything brew does, one click away

No mystery. Each button runs the exact brew command you would have typed — and shows you its output while it runs.

Updates

See what's outdated. Upgrade it your way.

133 packages behind? Upgrade everything, cherry-pick one, or snooze the noisy ones for a week.

  • Scheduled checks with notifications — every 6h, daily, or weekly
  • Automatic background upgrades that wait for AC power
  • Running apps are closed cleanly before their upgrade
  • The menu bar icon shows the outdated count at a glance
Live console

Watch Homebrew do its thing

A Cakebrew-style console docks at the bottom of the window. Install something and the real output streams in — colors, warnings, exit codes, a Stop button.

  • Every operation is queued, logged, and kept in History
  • Readable brew doctor, cleanup previews with sizes
  • Failures show the real error — not a shrug
Beyond Homebrew

Every app on your Mac, one inventory

Brewer scans everything — casks, App Store buys, Sparkle self-updaters, and apps you dragged in years ago — with size, source, and architecture.

  • Update Sparkle apps directly: download, back up the old version, install
  • Read the release notes before you say yes
  • Roll back from automatic backups if a new version misbehaves
  • Adopt manually-installed apps so brew manages them from now on
Uninstaller

Delete apps without the crumbs

Drop any app on Brewer and it finds the caches, preferences, containers and logs left behind — then trashes it all together. Casks go through brew, leftovers included.

  • Everything goes to the Trash, so nothing is lost forever
  • SmartDelete watches the Trash and offers cleanup automatically
  • System and Apple apps are protected — always
